# The Raz Code of Conduct

The Raz project aims to maintain a professional, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone involved.

- Keep discussions focused on the project’s goals and technical merits.
- Communicate respectfully and consider different perspectives.
- Provide constructive feedback and engage in good-faith technical discussion.
- Favor clear, simple solutions and respect the licenses and attribution requirements of any work you include. See `LICENSE` for this project’s license.

Project maintainers may step in when necessary to preserve a productive and professional environment.
